About

Most of what I do sits close to the hardware. On THEIA, the UMD Satellite Development Program's CubeSat, I lead the six-person Electrical Power System team and built the ground station. On TERP RAPTOR, a UMD CubeSat headed for a 2029 flyby of the asteroid Apophis, I'm designing the flight software interface to the attitude control system.

This past summer I was a systems engineering intern at Booz Allen Hamilton, where I built and validated a wire harness between two hardware systems and helped build a firmware deployment tool for industrial control systems. On my own time I'm building a quadcopter from the board up: flight computer, ESC, and bare-metal firmware.

I'm finishing a B.S. in Computer Engineering with a minor in Chinese (expected December 2028). Outside of engineering I'm usually learning a language, playing soccer, or finding something good to eat.

Design notes

Flight computer

A four-layer board carrying the STM32 and a six-axis IMU on high-speed SPI, with regulated power rails, controlled-impedance routing, and split ground planes.

ESC

A three-phase brushless controller: three half-bridge stages of N-channel MOSFETs behind gate drivers. The high-current polygon pours are laid out to keep the switching loops small and their parasitic inductance low.

Firmware

Bare-metal C with direct register access, DMA, and hardware timers for the PID stabilization loop and PWM motor output.

Debugging writeup · THEIA

The OBC-to-S-band pinout fault

During integration and test on THEIA, the S-band transmitter wouldn't transmit when commanded by the on-board computer. The cause was a pinout mismatch between the two boards.

  • AIT
  • Schematic tracing
  • Wire harnessing
  • RF
How I found it

Symptom

No RF output when the OBC commanded the transmitter. Its TX-enable line couldn't be reached from the OBC side of the interface, so the radio never keyed.

Diagnosis

I traced both boards' schematics pin by pin across the interface to find where the OBC's pinout and the transmitter's pinout disagreed.

Fix

I built a custom remapping harness that routes the OBC's signal to the transmitter's TX-enable pin. With the harness in place, the link worked.
